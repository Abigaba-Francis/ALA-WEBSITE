OUR ORIGIN STORY

The Amari Leaver's Association was founded in 2025 by students of Amari Christian Secondary School who shared a common experience: the challenge of transitioning out of care systems and into independent adulthood. Many of these students had grown up in foster care, orphanages, or under guardianship, and they understood firsthand the emotional, social, and financial hurdles that come with leaving care.

Rather than facing these challenges alone, they came together to form a support network one rooted in faith, friendship, and shared responsibility. What began as informal peer circles and prayer groups evolved into a structured association with leadership roles, outreach programs, and a growing impact within the school and surrounding community?
