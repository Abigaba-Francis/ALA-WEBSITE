Our origin story

Stronger because we stand together.

The Amari Leaver's Association was founded in 2025 by students of Amari Christian Secondary School who understood the challenge of leaving care and moving into independent adulthood.

Many had grown up in foster care, orphanages, or under guardianship. Rather than face those challenges alone, they created a support network grounded in faith, friendship, and shared responsibility.

What began as informal peer circles and prayer groups grew into an association with leadership roles, outreach programs, and a growing impact within the school and surrounding community.
